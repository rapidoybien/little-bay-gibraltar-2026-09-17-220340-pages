A family since 2016

Little Bay opened its doors in April 2016 in Ocean Village and has become one of Gibraltar's most celebrated culinary destinations, with a team that has spent years building its hospitality culture.

Welcome to Little Bay

On the Ocean Village promenade, Little Bay serves modern Indian cuisine made with fresh produce, tandoori specialities, curries, naans and aromatic rice dishes, with generous options for vegetarians, vegans and gluten-free guests.

A bar with its own soul

Little Bay's bar team crafts signature cocktails with fresh fruit, aromatic herbs and spices, perfect to enjoy before, during or after dinner.
