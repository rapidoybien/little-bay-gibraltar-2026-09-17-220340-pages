A Distinctive Experience

We welcome local residents, international visitors, business leaders and even members of the Royal Family. Our guests travel from the UK, Spain, Portugal, Morocco, the Middle East, India, Canada and the United States to experience our distinctive Indian cuisine, exceptional hospitality and award-winning dining.

Welcome to Little Bay

Since opening our doors in April 2016, Little Bay has become one of Gibraltar's most celebrated culinary destinations. Situated on the vibrant waterfront of Ocean Village, we offer freshly made modern Indian cuisine, with high quality across our dining room, bar and 30-table waterfront terrace, plus home delivery.

International Recognition

Named Gibraltar's Best Restaurant at the World Culinary Awards in 2022, 2023 and 2025. Proud holders of 1 AA Rosette since 2025 for our culinary standards. Winners of Best International Restaurant 2023 at the Asian Curry Awards, and Best Vegan-Friendly Indian Restaurant 2023 at the LuxLife Global Vegan Awards for our plant-based Indian cuisine.

Opening Hours

Open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Closed Saturdays and Sundays.
