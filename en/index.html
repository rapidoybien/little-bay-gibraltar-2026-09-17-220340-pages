Award-winning in Gibraltar

Named Gibraltar's Best Restaurant at the World Culinary Awards in 2022, 2023 and 2025, and holders of an AA Rosette since 2025, Little Bay has also been recognised as Best International Restaurant at the Asian Curry Awards and Best Vegan-Friendly Indian Restaurant at the LuxLife Global Vegan Awards. Consistent recognition of our kitchen and our team's hospitality.

Welcome to Little Bay

Since opening in April 2016, Little Bay has become one of Gibraltar's most celebrated culinary destinations. Situated on the waterfront of Ocean Village, we welcome locals, international visitors and lovers of Indian cuisine with a modern, thoughtful and flavour-packed offering.

The terrace and the cocktail bar

Enjoy our waterfront terrace at Ocean Village, overlooking the marina, or take a seat at the bar where our team, led by Head Mixologist Adam, crafts signature cocktails with fresh fruit, aromatic herbs and spices. A complete experience before, during or after dinner.
